# Mood Board — Heritage Badge

**Feeling:** earned, expeditionary, club/heritage — a crest you were awarded, not
bought.

**Palette (direction):** bone `#F2E9D8` · slate `#2B3A42` · olive `#5A5A3C` ·
one accent: ochre `#C8892B` *(≤3 inks + garment; muted, one warm accent — R-COL-01,
R-COL-05, R-COL-07)*

**Typography:** condensed grotesque or slab display, arced along the ring; wide-
tracked all-caps support; optional mono "EST. / coordinates" detail line. Two
faces max, clean symmetric arc. *(R-TYPE-01/02/04/05)*

**Texture / print style:** subtle ink mottle + light design-aware distress;
`printStyle.id: vintage` or `clean`. Process-emulated, never uniform grunge.
*(R-TEX-01/03/05, R-TREND-02)*

**Layout archetype:** centered emblem / circular seal — contained gestalt, generous
internal negative space. *(R-COMP-02, R-NEG-01)*

**Garment:** sand, bone, olive, or charcoal.

**References:** `reference_images/liked/tshirts/` (badge/emblem exemplars).

**Target genome:** `template: badge` · `palette.strategy: garmentAware|duotone` ·
`typography.case: upper`, `placement: top` (arc) · `printStyle.id: vintage` ·
single country / achievement set.

**Embodies:** R-VH-01 (one crest hero), R-COMP-02, R-TYPE-04, R-COL-07, R-STORY-01
(real est. date / coordinates), R-TREND-01.

**Avoid:** overcrowded ring interior; long names that break the arc; bevel/gloss on
the seal; more than one accent.

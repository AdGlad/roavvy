# Mood Board — Vintage Poster (WPA / National-Park)

**Feeling:** nostalgic, sun-faded, grand-travel — a mid-century park/expedition
poster reduced to a wearable graphic.

**Palette (direction):** cream `#EFE6D2` · faded teal `#3E7C78` · rust `#B4552E` ·
warm near-black `#241F1C` *(3–4 muted, era-coded inks; one temperature story —
R-COL-01/06/07/08)*

**Typography:** rounded slab or classic serif display; mixed or tracked caps; a
short tagline. Warmth over athletic. *(R-TYPE-03/05)*

**Texture / print style:** coarse rotated **halftone** for skies/fades + gentle
edge wear; `printStyle.id: halftone` or `sunFaded`. Fades are dots, never smooth
gradients. *(R-PRINT-01/02, R-TEX-01)*

**Layout archetype:** full-front simplified scene *or* framed poster — flat stacked
planes (mountains/landmarks), one hero subject, quiet lower third. *(R-VH-01,
R-ICON-03, R-NEG-01)*

**Garment:** cream, slate, dusty blue.

**References:** `reference_images/liked/tshirts/` (poster/scene exemplars).

**Target genome:** `template: landmark` (scene) or `badge` (framed) ·
`palette.strategy: duotone|monochrome` · `printStyle.id: halftone|sunFaded` ·
single-country or landmark set with a real notable site.

**Embodies:** R-COL-07, R-PRINT-01/02, R-ICON-03 (flat simplified), R-STORY-01
(real landmark/elevation), R-TREND-02/03.

**Avoid:** photoreal imagery; smooth gradients; more than ~4 colours; mixing this
with modern-line or heavy grunge.

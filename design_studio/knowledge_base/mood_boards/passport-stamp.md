# Mood Board — Passport Stamp / Collection

**Feeling:** collected, nostalgic, proof-of-journey — the ritual and residue of
travel, many places unified into one designed set.

**Palette (direction):** bone `#EFE6D2` + faded navy `#2E3A59` + one muted red-stamp
accent `#9E3B33`. Flags/stamps rendered in the shared tone, not literal colours.
*(R-COL-01/04, R-FLAG-01)*

**Typography:** mono/typewriter for dates & codes; a condensed display for the count
or title; tracked caps. *(R-TYPE-01/02/05, R-STORY-01)*

**Texture / print style:** ink-stamp mottle + light edge wear; `printStyle.id:
stamp` or `vintage`. Design-aware, subtle. *(R-TEX-01/03/05)*

**Layout archetype:** **uniform** stamp/badge collage grid — identical tokens, equal
gutters, one shared style; or a count-as-hero typographic lockup for very large
sets. Ruthless uniformity is what saves the grid. *(R-FLAG-03, R-COMP-02,
R-MERCH-02)*

**Garment:** bone, navy, sand.

**References:** `reference_images/liked/tshirts/` (collection/grid exemplars).

**Target genome:** `template: passport` (`stampMode: entryExit`) or
`grid`+`layoutMode: normalizedGrid|treemap` · `density: balanced` ·
`palette.strategy: monochrome|duotone` · `printStyle.id: stamp` · lifetime/year/
multiCountry set.

**Embodies:** R-FLAG-03 (uniform grid, normalised aspect), R-MERCH-02 (density
tiers by count), R-STORY-02 (journey/collection), R-COL-01, R-TREND-01.

**Avoid:** full-colour flag pile with different sizes/shadows; replicating real
official stamp artwork (abstract it); clutter without a strict grid; sensitive-flag
adjacency handled carelessly (R-FLAG-05).

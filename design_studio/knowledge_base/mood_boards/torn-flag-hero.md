# Mood Board — Torn Flag Hero

**Feeling:** bold, worn, lived-in — a single national flag *is* the whole graphic,
ragged and weathered. The studio's best-calibrated liked exemplar.

**Palette (direction):** the single flag's own colours, **tamed/desaturated**, on a
neutral garment; or a mono tattered flag (one ink). Surround stays neutral.
*(R-COL-04/07, R-FLAG-01 exception: single-flag hero)*

**Typography:** usually **none** — the flag carries it. If any, a small tracked
caps mark only. *(R-VH-01, R-TYPE-02)*

**Texture / print style:** hard, clearly-ripped edges + heavy design-aware
distress; `printStyle.id: edgeTear` (+ distress). Subtractive wear that reveals the
garment; one coherent noise field. *(R-TEX-01/02/04, R-PRINT-*)*

**Layout archetype:** single hero, bold scale, centred, generous negative space —
sparse. *(R-VH-01, R-NEG-01)*

**Garment:** black, white, heather.

**References:** `reference_images/liked/torn/` (`torn-through-usa`,
`torn-vertical-usa`, `usa-torn-color-bw`) — analysed in
`../../reference_analysis/torn-flag-tshirts.json` (focalConcentration 0.85,
focalHierarchy strong, legibility high).

**Target genome:** `template: grid` · `layoutMode: packedRow` · `density: sparse` ·
`clip.shape: none` · single country · `printStyle.id: edgeTear` (+ distress).
Curated recipes: `recipes/torn_flag_usa`, `torn_flag_mono`, `ripped_flag`.

**Embodies:** R-VH-01, R-FLAG-01 (single-flag exception), R-TEX-01/02, R-NEG-01,
R-COMP-03 (reads at a glance).

**Avoid:** combining multiple torn flags; jagged *random* grunge rips (a real rip
tells a story); losing flag recognizability under the wear.

**Note:** a third "ripped-through-fabric" look (flag seen through a claw gash in the
shirt) needs a fabric-tear mask the renderer does not yet have — a capability gap,
not a style to fake with grunge.

# Research Summary — Premium Travel/Adventure Apparel & Buyer Psychology

*Stream 1 of 4. Distilled research; principles only, no copied artwork. Informs
rules R-COL-01/07, R-ICON-03, R-VH-01, R-COMP-02, R-STORY-01/03/04, R-TREND-01/02,
R-MERCH-01. Source links at end.*

## Core finding

Two structural advantages define premium travel apparel, and Roavvy has both:
**real personal data** rendered through **a coherent design system**. Stock travel
merch has neither, which is why it reads generic. Every principle below protects
one of those two pillars.

## Key principles (condensed)

1. **Tight, era-coded palette (2–5 inks).** The vintage-poster / screen-print
   heritage was born from a physical constraint (cost per colour); that constraint
   became the *signal* of intentional design. Muted, desaturated earths read as
   sun-aged = authentic. → R-COL-01, R-COL-07.
2. **Flat colour; no photorealism / bevels / gradient-as-crutch.** Simplification
   is editorial judgement — the labour amateurs and naive AI skip. Bevel/gloss are
   the 2000s clip-art tell. Halftone/duotone photo treatments are fine because
   they *convert* a photo into a graphic. → R-ICON-03, R-TREND-02.
3. **One hero + clear focal hierarchy.** The eye needs an entry point; equal-weight
   elements read as "a pile of assets." → R-VH-01.
4. **Type as a designed system, not a label.** Default/garbled fonts are the #1
   cheap/AI tell; era-coded pairing does half the storytelling. → R-TYPE-05/06.
5. **Earned texture, not decorative grunge.** "Wear that could physically have
   happened" (halftone, registration offset, edge crack) reads premium; random
   splatter reads template. → R-TEX-01.
6. **Deliberate containing geometry** (badge/crest/frame/grid) signals craft and
   gives the generator a layout skeleton. → R-COMP-02.
7. **Ground in a real, specific datum** (coords, dates, elevations, accurate
   outline). Specificity = proof-of-experience = why it gets worn. Wrong facts are
   worse than none. → R-STORY-01.
8. **Recognizable, accurate silhouettes** — recognition ("that's MY country") is
   the emotional payload; test at thumbnail scale. → R-ICON-04.
9. **Negative space + grid alignment** — crowding-to-edges and misalignment are
   the canonical amateur tells. → R-NEG-01, R-COMP-02.
10. **One coherent style per piece; no mashups.** Bundle palette+type+texture+
    container as locked presets. → R-TREND-01.
11. **Design for the wearer's identity claim**, and prefer **understated insider
    cues** over loud tourist branding — the premium/souvenir divide is mostly
    volume. → R-STORY-03/04.
12. **Controlled variation, not randomness**, and **garment-aware print reality**
    (contrast, safe area, min stroke). → R-MERCH-01, R-COL-02/03.

## Premium vs souvenir-shop (mechanism table)

| Dimension | Premium / heritage | Cheap / souvenir |
|---|---|---|
| Palette | 2–5 muted, era-coded | Many high-saturation, RGB-bright |
| Rendering | Flat, simplified | Photoreal clip-art, bevels, gloss |
| Type | Curated pairing, tracked, on-path | Default/mixed/garbled |
| Hierarchy | One hero + support | Equal-weight clutter |
| Texture | Physically plausible wear | Random grunge / dead-flat |
| Containment | Deliberate badge/grid/frame | Floating, edge-crowded |
| Specificity | Real coords/dates/outlines | Generic, wrong details |
| Message | Understated insider cue | Loud "I ♥ PLACE" |
| Coherence | One committed style | Mashup |
| Print prep | Garment-aware, safe-area | Ignores garment, hairlines |

## Buyer motivation → visual choice

| Why they buy/wear | Visual choice that delivers it |
|---|---|
| Identity signalling ("I'm an explorer") | Committed style + emblem/crest + confident understatement |
| Memory / nostalgia | Faded palette, print wear, real date/coords |
| Proof / earned status | Accurate outlines, visit-count, correct coords |
| Belonging / tribe | Recognizable-at-distance silhouette + insider cue |
| Taste display | Restraint: limited palette, space, alignment, coherence |
| Status ("traveler not tourist") | Muted premium colourways, subtle over loud |
| Collectibility | Curated variation as a designed collection |

## Dated/cheap treatments to AVOID
Bevel-emboss & drop shadows; rainbow saturation; random grunge splatter; default/
mixed fonts & garbled pseudo-text; photorealistic images on a tee; equal-weight
clutter; edge crowding; misalignment; style mashups; loud tourist slogans;
invented/wrong details; hairlines & garment-blind contrast.
